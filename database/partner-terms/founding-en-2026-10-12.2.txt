The Early Withdrawal Charge equals $200 multiplied by the number of contractual months remaining in the six-month Term, subject to a maximum of $900. Withdrawal exactly after three completed months results in a $600 charge. No Early Withdrawal Charge applies after all six months have been completed.

For withdrawal between monthly anniversaries, the remaining Term is the sum of the full contractual months following the next anniversary and the unexpired fraction of the current contractual month. That fraction equals the calendar days remaining until the next anniversary divided by the total calendar days in the current contractual month. Multiply that sum by the monthly rate, apply the maximum charge, and round the final amount once to the nearest cent. The effective withdrawal date is the first day on which Partner no longer participates. For example, two full remaining months plus 15 remaining days in a 30-day contractual month result in a $500 charge.

ITC will provide an invoice showing the relevant dates, rate, remaining months and days, proration and applicable maximum. Payment is due on the effective withdrawal date. This charge relates to early withdrawal and is not a balance of unpaid monthly invitation fees. No separate recovery of the invitation discount will be added for the same withdrawal, and double recovery is not permitted. Liability for separate violations remains unaffected. The $300 initial participation fee is not credited again against the Early Withdrawal Charge and is not refundable upon voluntary withdrawal.
