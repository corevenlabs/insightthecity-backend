# Privacy Policy

Insight The City and ITC Club

**Last updated:** October 31, 2026

# 1. Who We Are and the Scope of This Policy

Eventland LLC, based in New Jersey, United States, operates the Insight The City app, the ITC Club membership services, and the insightthecity.com (https://insightthecity.com) website. In this policy, “ITC,” “we,” “us,” and “our services” refer to these services and their operating entity.

This policy explains what personal data we process, how we obtain it, why we use it, who we share it with, how long we retain it, and how you can exercise your rights. It applies to app and website users, ITC Club members, subscribers to our communications, and representatives of businesses that work with us.

For privacy questions or requests, contact **info@insightthecity.com**.

This policy provides information about our practices. Publishing or reading it does not replace any specific permissions we must request for particular processing activities.

# 2. What Information We Process and Why

**Account and authentication.** We collect the name, email address, and password you provide to create an account. We also process your language, account identifiers, account administration dates, and records of acceptance of the relevant legal documents.

We use this data to create and manage your account, authenticate you, restore access, provide support, and manage the features available to you. Passwords are stored using verification hashes.

**Profile and preferences.** You may add a photo, your usual area, and interests. We use this information to maintain your profile and tailor the language, content, and recommendations. Member profiles do not function as public social profiles.

Authorized personnel may access the information needed to provide support, administer the service, or investigate incidents.

**Membership and payments.** We process membership status, customer and subscription identifiers, transaction references, amounts, currencies, billing intervals, membership periods, and information about cancellations and receipts.

This data enables us to manage access to ITC Club, handle charges and renewals, resolve issues, and meet accounting and legal obligations.

Full payment card details are entered through Stripe's services. Our integration retains payment references and statuses rather than the full card number or security code. Receipts provided by Stripe may contain billing details and partial payment method information.

**Optional Link checkout.** Link is Stripe's service for saving and reusing payment information. When you use Link, contact and billing details may include your email address, phone number, and billing address. Stripe or Link may make these details, together with partial payment method information, available to ITC through payment records. We may use the information made available to us to manage or verify membership payments and subscriptions, resolve payment issues, prevent misuse, and meet accounting and legal obligations.

Using Link is optional. Its applicable terms and privacy notice are presented in the payment flow. Choosing Link is separate from authorizing the ITC Club subscription and its recurring charges. You can use another payment option offered at checkout without registering for Link.

**Benefits and redemptions.** We process the selected benefit and business, the redemption code or token, and records of its issuance, expiration, status, and use. We use this information to validate benefits, prevent misuse, and prepare internal activity statistics. QR codes are linked to your account in our systems as explained in Section 6.

The expiration of a QR code, including its 24-hour usage period, does not determine when the associated record is deleted.

**Conversations with Yorki.** We process your messages, generated responses, and the context needed to respond and maintain conversation continuity. Section 4 explains how information is shared with OpenAI.

**Location.** When you use nearby-place features and grant the relevant permission, the app processes your device's coordinates only on the device. These coordinates are never saved, sent to ITC's servers, or shared with third parties. A neighborhood, address, business, or other reference you voluntarily enter is processed separately; if you include it in a Yorki message, Section 4 applies.

**Communications and support.** We process the email address you provide to receive news, things to do, and promotions, your subscription status, and the data needed to manage delivery. When you contact us, we process your contact details and the content of your request to respond.

**Businesses and representatives.** We process information about businesses and their representatives, such as names, email addresses, phone numbers, language, collaboration terms, offered benefits, materials, images, billing information, receipts, and business activity.

We use this information to manage the collaboration, publish the business profile and benefit, administer payments, and maintain operational communications. Images, descriptions, and business details approved for publication are displayed as public content about the business.

**Legal acceptances.** We retain records of acceptance of terms, including the account or signatory reference, date, version, and accepted document, as well as renewal records where applicable. We use these records to establish the applicable terms and address claims.

**Technical data.** Our services and providers may generate information such as IP addresses, dates and times, browsers, operating systems, requested resources, errors, and security events. We use this information to deliver content, maintain sessions, protect the service, and resolve issues.

We also receive information from providers needed to administer their functions, such as payment confirmations, subscription updates, or email delivery results.

# 3. Location, Local Storage, and Biometrics

**Location.** The app requests location access when relevant to a feature, such as searching for nearby places. It uses foreground location and is not designed to track your movements in the background.

Coordinates may be precise, depending on your device and the permission granted. They are processed temporarily and exclusively on your device. The app never saves these coordinates on the device or elsewhere and does not send them to ITC's servers, OpenAI, Google Maps, Google Places, or any other third party.

You may deny permission and search using a written reference. You can also change the permission in your device settings. Revoking permission prevents new coordinates from being obtained through that access. No saved device-location coordinates need to be deleted because the app does not retain them. Information you voluntarily enter in a conversation is handled separately under Section 4.

**Local storage.** The app stores session information and certain preferences, favorites, and searches on your device. Some local data may exist even if you have not created an account. This storage does not include coordinates obtained from your device's location service.

**Biometric unlocking.** If you enable Face ID or fingerprint unlocking, your device's system performs the verification. The app uses the result to allow unlocking and retains your preference; it does not receive the biometric templates used by the system.

# 4. Yorki and the Use of OpenAI

# What Yorki Does

Yorki is an artificial intelligence assistant that uses the OpenAI API to answer questions about things to do, weather, benefits, discounts, and itinerary planning.

**Yorki requires an account and sign-in. Users without an account or who are not signed in cannot use Yorki. Yorki is free to use and does not require a paid ITC Club membership.** Its use also requires the separate consent to share information with OpenAI described below.

Its responses are generated automatically and may contain errors or outdated information. Check relevant details with the business, organizer, or appropriate source before booking, purchasing, or traveling.

# What Information Is Sent

To generate responses, our integration shares the following with OpenAI:

- Your profile name.
- Your configured language.
- Your membership status.
- The message you send and context from up to the latest 50 messages available within the 90-day conversation window.
- Information about places relevant to the search, where applicable.

Coordinates obtained from your device's location service are not sent to OpenAI. If you include personal data, addresses, travel details, or other information in your messages, that content may also be included in what is sent to OpenAI.

You do not need to provide passwords, full payment card numbers, identity documents, or sensitive information to receive recommendations. Avoid including other people's personal data that is unnecessary for your request.

# Your Choice Before Information Is Sent

Before information is shared with OpenAI, the app allows you to **accept or decline** the processing needed to use Yorki.

**Unless and until you accept, our integration does not send your messages or the context data described above to OpenAI.** If you decline, Yorki will not be able to generate responses through that service.

Authorization for Yorki is handled specifically and is not replaced by merely accepting the general terms or reading this policy.

You may request withdrawal of your consent by emailing **info@insightthecity.com** with the subject **“Withdraw Yorki consent”** and providing the email address associated with your account. We will process your request and confirm that it has been addressed. While it is being processed, you can avoid new transmissions by not using Yorki.

Withdrawal applies to future processing based on that consent. You may also request deletion of conversations we retain, subject to the exceptions explained in this policy.

# History and Retention

ITC retains conversations to maintain service continuity, with a 90-day accessible history window and periodic processes to remove older messages.

Opening an empty conversation, signing out, or uninstalling the app does not by itself delete the history stored on our servers.

Our integration uses **store=false** so that responses are not saved as retrievable history through the OpenAI API. This setting does not mean zero retention across all of the provider's systems.

Under OpenAI's terms and documentation, data sent to its API is not used to train its models by default unless the customer opts in. Abuse prevention and security logs may be retained for up to 30 days by default, with legal or harm-prevention exceptions and other technical retention depending on the feature used. See OpenAI's data controls (https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/guides/your-data).

# 9. How Long We Retain Information

We retain data for as long as necessary to provide the service, fulfill the purposes described, and meet applicable obligations. Retention periods depend on the type of information and the system in which it is processed.

**Category - Retention period or criterion**

- **Account and profile:** For as long as the account exists. Inactivity does not by itself trigger automatic deletion.
- **Device-location coordinates:** Never retained. They are processed only on your device, are not saved, and are not transmitted to ITC's servers or third parties.
- **Conversations with Yorki:** Accessible history within a 90-day window, with periodic, progressive removal of older messages.
- **Membership, transactions, and redemptions:** For as long as needed to administer the account and service; linked records are included in the deletion process, except where retention is legally required or justified.
- **Business and representative data:** For as long as needed to manage the business relationship and afterward for legal or accounting obligations or justified claims.
- **Promotional communications:** While the subscription is active. After unsubscribing, minimal information may be retained to honor suppression requests, document permissions, or address claims.
- **Sent or canceled business emails:** Recipient details and content are cleaned up after 90 days. Pending or failed messages are managed to complete delivery or resolve issues.
- **Ordinary backend logs in Google Cloud Logging:** Configured retention of 30 days.
- **Certain mandatory Google Cloud audit logs:** Retention of 400 days.
- **Completed deletion requests:** Their identifiers are cleared, and the minimal process record is deleted after 30 days. Requests requiring retries remain under management until resolved.
- **Google Analytics:** Configured for two months for event data and 14 months for user data. The user identifier's period resets with new activity. These settings do not by themselves determine retention of aggregated reports.
- **Legal evidence, billing, and accounting:** For as long as required by law or necessary and proportionate to establish terms, prevent fraud, or address claims.

Registrations stored in WordPress and Mailchimp data are managed separately. You can request their deletion through our privacy email address; unsubscribing from a mailing does not automatically purge all systems.

**Backups and recovery.** The app database is configured to retain up to eight automatic backups and recovery logs for up to seven days. Eight backups do not necessarily mean a maximum of eight days. Manual maintenance backups may also exist.

Primary file storage temporarily retains deleted objects through a seven-day soft-deletion period. Automatic backups of the website hosted on SiteGround cover approximately one month.

Backups may contain data deleted from the active system until those backups are replaced or deleted according to their purpose and cycle. Deletion from the active system, backups, and provider records does not necessarily occur at the same time.

Stripe and other providers may retain records under their own obligations and terms. OpenAI retention is explained in Section 4.

When a deletion request is subject to an exception, we will explain the categories we must retain and the justification, where applicable.

# 10. Membership Cancellation and Account Deletion

**Canceling your membership.** You can manage cancellation under **Profile > My Membership**. Canceling renewal does not automatically delete the account or associated records.

**Deleting your account.** You can start the process using the account deletion feature in the app or our account deletion page (https://itc-backend-1031252664334.us-east1.run.app/delete-account?lang=es), without having the app installed. The page verifies your email address and asks you to confirm deletion.

You may also email **info@insightthecity.com** with the subject **“Delete my account”** and provide the associated email address. Do not send your password.

The process:

- **1.** Records the request and blocks access to the account.
- **2.** Attempts to cancel the linked subscription in Stripe.
- **3.** Deletes the account, conversations, and applicable linked records, and manages deletion of associated files.
- **4.** Sends confirmation when the process is complete.

In the app, this process also clears account-linked local data on the device from which the feature is used.

If Stripe does not confirm cancellation or another failure occurs, the request may remain pending for a retry. Blocked access does not by itself establish that the subscription has been canceled. Contact us if you do not receive confirmation or still see an active subscription so we can review its status.

Deletion is subject to the legal exceptions and backup cycles described in this policy. Where a legal deadline applies to the request, we will comply with it and explain any permitted extension and its reason.

**Signing out or uninstalling the app does not cancel your membership or delete your account.**
